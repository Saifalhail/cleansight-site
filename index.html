Privacy Scan

Find photos with private details.

Privacy Scan suggests photos that may show IDs, cards, passwords or verification codes. You decide what stays and what goes to the Vault, behind Face ID.

It reads English text only, and it can miss items or flag ordinary photos.

Watch Mode

Stay clean without rescanning.

Turn on Watch Mode and each night's new photos are checked on the device. You get one short review a week. It only suggests: nothing leaves Photos until you approve it.

Questions

Straight answers.

Do my photos leave my phone?

No. Finding duplicates, spotting blur and reading the text in screenshots all happen on your iPhone, and there is no CleanSight account. Usage statistics, crash reports and purchase data are sent, as the privacy policy describes. A photo never is.

What is free and what needs Pro?

Scanning is free. Opening what a scan found, and every action that removes or rewrites something, needs Pro.

Does deleting free up space straight away?

Not on any app. iOS keeps deleted photos in Recently Deleted for 30 days, and CleanSight says so instead of claiming the space is already free.

Can it delete something I wanted to keep?

Not without you. Bulk deletion always has a review step, and iOS holds removed photos in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

What about photos stored in iCloud?

CleanSight marks which originals are in iCloud and which are on the device, so you know what a delete affects.
