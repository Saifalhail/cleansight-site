CleanSight Data and Deletion

Effective date: September 20, 2026

CleanSight does not create a CleanSight account and does not upload your photo library, recognized text, contacts, or vault files to CleanSight servers.

Delete information stored on your device

  • Delete scan history or cached analysis using the available controls in CleanSight.
  • Delete or restore vault items from the vault. Restore anything you want to keep before deleting the app.
  • Delete CleanSight from your device to remove its app container, including remaining local history, settings, and vault files.
  • Manage Photos and Contacts separately in the relevant Apple apps. Deleting CleanSight does not delete content that remains in those apps.

Deletion is permanent for app-only data and vault files once the app container is removed. CleanSight cannot remotely recover them.

Analytics, diagnostics, and purchases

Firebase, RevenueCat, and Apple process limited technical, usage, diagnostic, and purchase information as described in the CleanSight Privacy Policy. Because CleanSight has no account and uses provider-generated identifiers, we may not be able to identify a provider record from your name or email alone.

You may contact privacy@cleansight.ai to exercise an applicable privacy right concerning support correspondence or other information held by BITESIGHT LTD. We may request reasonable information to verify and locate the request. Apple subscription and purchase records are managed through your Apple Account.
