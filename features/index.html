Features

Everything CleanSight finds — and what it does about it.

Everything it finds, one review flow, zero uploads. Everything below happens on your iPhone — and once it's clean, it stays clean.

Photos

The clutter

Exact duplicates

Identical copies, grouped with the best one pre-selected as the keeper.

Similar photos

Different angle, lighting or filter — the shots Apple's Duplicates album can't group. Compare side by side, drag to swap, keep one.

Blurry shots

Blur detection with Strict / Normal / Lenient sensitivity. Motion blur, missed focus, shaky hands.

Dark photos

Catches the black frames and accidental pocket shots.

Screenshots that know what they are

Sorted into codes, boarding passes, tickets, directions, receipts and chats, each with an idea of when it expired — so the ones that have done their job surface first.

Screen recordings

Usually the largest, least-watched files on the phone.

Bursts

Keep the best frame of every burst — CleanSight picks it, you confirm.

Live Photos

Convert to stills and drop the hidden 3-second video attached to each one.

Social media saves

Downloads from WhatsApp, Instagram and friends — no camera metadata, no GPS, easy to spot.

Large files

Sorted by size, with the ☁︎ / 📱 mark so you know which deletes actually free space on the phone.

Similar videos

Near-duplicate clips grouped together — the three takes of the same moment.

Video compression

Max, Balanced or Light compression. Preview the result and choose keep-both or replace.

Auto-categories

People, pets, food, nature, memes, selfies — on-device classification, browsable and swipeable.

Stays clean

Clean once. Then let the phone keep watch.

Watch Mode

Every night, the day's new photos are checked on the phone — duplicates, blur, screenshots that have expired. One short review a week, a two-minute swipe. Verification-code screenshots are flagged the same day. It only suggests; nothing goes without you.

Deletion receipts

Every removal is written down — names, dates, sizes, thumbnails — and every duplicate group explains why the keeper was kept. Trust it because you can check it.

Calendar cleanup

Spam invites, duplicate events, declined leftovers, ghost recurrences and junk subscribed calendars. Review the list, remove what you choose.

Privacy

The things you'd rather not have in there

Privacy Scan

Finds photos that may contain IDs, payment cards, passwords or one-time codes. You review every suggestion — nothing is auto-deleted, nothing is uploaded, and a repeat scan takes seconds because unchanged photos aren't read again.

{{icon:location-off:20}}

Share without location

Send a copy with the GPS position removed and everything else intact — from any photo list, or as a Shortcut.

Vault

Copies behind Face ID, Touch ID or passcode, stored with complete file protection on the device only. Originals stay in Photos until you remove them yourself. Vault access survives the end of a subscription.

The way you review

Built for deciding, not scrolling

Swipe

Left removes, right keeps, up files to an album, down favourites. Haptics on every decision, shake to undo.

Grid

Same session, tap to stage. Switch between grid and swipe any time.

Month by month

A finishable goal per month and a completion percentage for the whole library.

iCloud aware

Every thumbnail shows where the original lives. Filter any list by On iPhone / iCloud.

Beyond photos

The rest of the phone

Contacts

Duplicate and incomplete contacts, merged or removed with one review.

Documents

Point it at a Files folder: large files, old files, duplicates, with iCloud Drive state shown.

The weekly ledger

What arrived, what you cleaned, and a storage forecast. Widgets, Lock Screen and Dynamic Island included.
