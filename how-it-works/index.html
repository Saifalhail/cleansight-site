How it works

Your photos never leave your iPhone. Here is how.

"Private" is an easy word to print on a landing page. This is what it actually means for CleanSight, in the order things happen.

1

You grant Photos access

Full library access is needed because the job is library-wide: duplicates can't be found one photo at a time. Limited selection works too.

2

Your phone does the work

Every photo and video is analysed right on the iPhone — near-duplicates, blur, dark shots, categories and the text in screenshots. No photo is sent anywhere. Photos stored in iCloud are checked from their on-phone preview and never downloaded during a scan.

3

Results stay on your phone

What CleanSight learns about your library is kept on the device, so the next scan only looks at what changed and finishes in seconds. There is no CleanSight account and no photo upload.

4

You decide

Every group is reviewed. Deletions go through Apple's own Photos confirmation, land in Recently Deleted for 30 days, and CleanSight tells you so.

5

What does leave the phone

Usage statistics, crash reports and purchase data, plus the options you pick if you answer an in-app survey. Never a photo, a thumbnail or anything read from one. The privacy policy has the full list.
