How it works

Nothing leaves your phone. Here's the proof.

"Private" is an easy word to print on a landing page. This is what it actually means for CleanSight, in the order things happen.

1

You grant Photos access

Full library access is needed because the job is library-wide: duplicates can't be found one photo at a time. Limited selection works too, with the app staying fully functional.

2

Analysis runs on the Neural Engine

Vision feature prints for similarity, Laplacian variance for blur, luminance for dark shots, classification for categories, OCR for Privacy Scan. All Apple frameworks, all on the device. Cloud-only originals are analysed from their on-phone preview and never downloaded during a scan.

3

Results stay in a local database

Feature prints and scores are cached on the phone (SwiftData) so the next scan is incremental. There is no CleanSight account and no server that has ever seen a photo.

4

You decide

Every group is reviewed. Deletions go through Apple's own Photos confirmation, land in Recently Deleted for 30 days, and CleanSight tells you so — with the real number, not a fantasy one.

5

What does leave the phone

Anonymous usage events and crash reports (Firebase, no advertising identifier) and purchase receipts (RevenueCat). That's the complete list, and it's the same list in the privacy policy.
